# Model Assumptions

Every candidate model must have a dated definition, assumptions, success criteria, and falsification conditions before its target observable is evaluated.

## Classification

- **A1 (User):** Explicitly provided by the researcher.
- **A2 (Empirical):** Supported by direct observation or computation within the model.
- **A3 (Literature):** Drawn from peer-reviewed sources.
- **A4 (Model):** Internal to the approximation scheme.

All conclusions must cite applicable assumptions.

# Established Physics Constraints

Every candidate theory must be checked against the following. Failure of a necessary condition rejects the candidate for that regime.

## Requirements

- **General covariance.**
- **Energy-momentum conservation.** (Derived by variation of the action; verify identities.)
- **Stability and absence of unphysical degrees of freedom** within the claimed regime.
- **Causality and the appropriate initial-value problem.**
- **Recovery of known classical limits.**
- **Quantum-field-theoretic consistency** within the approximation used.
- **Equivalence-principle and solar-system constraints** where relevant.
- **Gravitational-wave constraints** where relevant.
- **Existing laboratory, atomic, and astrophysical observations.**

## Effective Field Theory

Use effective field theory appropriately. Higher-order terms are not automatically pathological. Their presence alone is not evidence for new physics.

Where a calculation is outside the controlled regime of the model, label it as such rather than extrapolating confidently.

## Baseline

Reproduce established results (GR, QFT effective actions, spectral geometry on graphs) before introducing new terms.

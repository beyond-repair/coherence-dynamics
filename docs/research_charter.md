# Research Charter

**Project:** Coherence Dynamics
**Version:** 0.1.0
**Date:** 2026-10-28

## Mission

Investigate whether coarse-graining, physical correlations, and unresolved degrees of freedom can produce a derived effective response with observable consequences, while preserving established physics in tested regimes.

The motivating intuition is that quantum, collective, and gravitational phenomena may share structural principles involving correlations, effective descriptions, and scale-dependent response.

**Hypothesis status: UNRESOLVED.** Do not assume correctness. Derive the mathematics necessary to test it.

## Core Principle

Derive first. Calculate second. Compare with observation third.

The project must not assume a universal numerical constant, a new force, modified gravity, or an informational source of energy. All physical effects must emerge from explicitly defined equations.

## Starting Framework

Begin with established general relativity and quantum field theory.

Investigate effective actions obtained by integrating out specified degrees of freedom:

\[
e^{i\Gamma_\mu[\phi,g]} = \int \mathcal{D}\chi\, e^{iS[\phi,\chi,g]},
\]

where \(\phi\) are retained fields, \(\chi\) are unresolved fields, \(g_{\mu\nu}\) is the spacetime metric, and \(\Gamma_\mu\) is the resulting effective action at scale \(\mu\).

This is a framework to instantiate, not a completed derivation.

## Distinctions Required

Carefully distinguish:

- Classical coarse-graining.
- Quantum effective actions.
- Statistical correlations.
- Quantum entanglement.
- Nonlocal response and memory.
- Emergent collective degrees of freedom.
- Genuine gravitational modifications.

These concepts must not be treated as interchangeable without mathematical justification.

## Stages

- **Stage A:** Audit and formalization.
- **Stage B:** Baseline reproduction.
- **Stage C:** Effective-action derivation.
- **Stage D:** Consistency and falsification.
- **Stage E:** Observable prediction.
- **Stage F:** Independent validation.
- **Stage G:** Cross-scale extension.

## Evidence Labels

Used consistently: ESTABLISHED, DERIVED, REPRODUCED, SUPPORTED, HYPOTHESIS, FAILED, UNRESOLVED.

Record failed hypotheses. Do not delete them.

## Ware Constant

Historical context only from prior repositories. Must not appear as a fixed parameter, target, normalization, optimization objective, or acceptance criterion in the new theory.
